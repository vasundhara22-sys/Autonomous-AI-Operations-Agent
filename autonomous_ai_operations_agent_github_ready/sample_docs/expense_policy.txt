Business Expense Policy

Eligible expenses. Travel, lodging, client meals, and approved training fees are reimbursable. Personal entertainment and fines are not reimbursable.

Receipts. Original receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses without a receipt above 500 are rejected unless the employee provides a signed declaration and the manager approves it.

Reimbursement timeline. Approved travel expenses are reimbursed within 10 working days of approval. Other expenses are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll run after approval.

Limits. Hotel stays are capped at 4,500 per night in metro cities and 3,000 elsewhere. Meals during travel are capped at 1,200 per day. Limits are reviewed every January.

Approval. Expenses under 25,000 are approved by the line manager. Expenses between 25,000 and 100,000 also need the finance business partner. Anything above 100,000 needs the head of department.
