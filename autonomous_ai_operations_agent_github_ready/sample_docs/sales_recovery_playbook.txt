Sales Recovery Playbook

Purpose. This playbook describes what regional managers must do when a region's weekly sales fall below the target threshold.

Trigger. A region is in recovery when its weekly achievement is below 80 percent of target. A region below 60 percent is in severe recovery and must be escalated.

Week one actions. The regional manager reviews the pipeline with the sales team and confirms coverage of open deals. The manager identifies the three largest deals at risk and agrees a next step for each within two working days.

Week two actions. The manager runs a conversion review of leads that did not progress past the first call. Any product that is out of stock is escalated to supply planning the same day.

Severe recovery. Regions in severe recovery have a review with the head of sales within 48 hours. The review decides whether reallocating reps from a stronger region is needed.

Reporting. The regional manager sends a recovery update every Friday until the region returns above 80 percent for two consecutive weeks. Updates include pipeline value, deals closed, and blockers.
