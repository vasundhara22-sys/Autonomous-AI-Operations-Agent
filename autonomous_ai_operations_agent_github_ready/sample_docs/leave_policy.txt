Annual Leave and Sick Leave Policy

Annual leave. Every full-time employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.5 days per month. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried into the next year. Any leave above that carry-over limit is lost on 31 March.

Requesting leave. Annual leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal. Requests of more than 5 consecutive days need written approval from the regional manager and HR.

Sick leave. Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave is approved by the employee's direct line manager, who must be told by 10:00 on the first day of absence.

Parental leave. Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 2 weeks. Parental leave is approved by HR and does not reduce annual leave.
